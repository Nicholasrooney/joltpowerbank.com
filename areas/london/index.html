Questions venues in London ask

Does it cost my business anything?

No. The station, power banks, installation and maintenance are free. You provide a small space and a standard power socket.

How does my venue earn from it?

Jolt runs a revenue share model. Your venue receives a share of the rental income generated by the station. We'll go through the figures with you before anything is installed.

Who looks after the station?

We do. Jolt installs the station, keeps it stocked and handles any customer support. Your staff don't need to do anything.

What happens if a customer doesn't return a power bank?

Every rental is secured with a card pre-authorisation, so the cost of an unreturned power bank is covered by the customer, not your venue.
