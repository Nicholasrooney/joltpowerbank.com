How it works

Relax.
We've got you
covered.

1. Tap

Tap your card or phone on the station to rent a power bank.

2. Charge

Each 6000mAh power bank has Lightning, USB-C and Micro-USB cables built in.

3. Return

Drop it back into any Jolt station when you're done.
