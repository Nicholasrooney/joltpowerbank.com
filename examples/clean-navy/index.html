Jolt is a power bank rental network for pubs, bars, restaurants, hotels and other venues. We install a free station, your guests tap a card to borrow a charger and stay longer, and your venue earns a share of every rental.

How much does a Jolt station cost a venue?

Nothing. The station, power banks, installation and maintenance are free. You provide a small space and a standard power socket, and Jolt shares the rental income with your venue.
