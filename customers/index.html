Our customers

Over 4,000 venues have already used Jolt: pubs, hotels, hospitals, nightclubs, stadiums and more. Here are some of them.

Showcase options:1. Orbit2. Drum3. Categories4. Flip wall5. Runway
Option 1

Orbit

Logos circling a Jolt station on tilted rings. The best-known venues are the biggest, at the front.

Option 2

Drum

Rows of venues roll over a cylinder toward you, each logo paired with the venue name and location.

Option 3

Categories

Counts by venue type with a grid for each. Cycles on its own; visitors can tap a category.

Option 4

Flip wall

A grid of tiles; one flips to a new venue every moment. Calm, readable, and shows a lot at once.

Option 5

Runway

Columns of venues streaming toward you on a tilted floor, out of the distance.
